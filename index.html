TouchSpark

2 Player Couples Arcade Game

Download on the App Store

TouchSpark turns your phone into a two-player arcade for date night. Lay the device between you, split the screen, and start playing — no second phone, no setup required!

Four Games on One Screen

Quick Draw

Both players hold a thumb on the screen. Wait through the suspenseful silence, then lift the moment the signal fires! Reflexes are measured in milliseconds — lift early or later and you lose the round instantly.

Finger Twister

Target rings spawn across both halves of the screen. Cover them all and hold on while your fingers tangle with your date's. First one to slip or let go loses.

Energy Rush

Energy orbs spawn in the middle of the screen. Drag them home to your base. First to 15 points wins.

Finger Race

Two mirrored winding tracks. Guide your ball along the course without touching the edges. One slip, and you're back to the start.

For the Loser (Date Forfeits)

Every round ends with a fun forfeit draw:

  • Feed your date a bite of dessert
  • Hold eye contact for 10 seconds without laughing
  • Winner picks the next date spot
  • Completely customizable — add your own secret tasks!

Good to Know

  • Face-to-Face or Side-by-Side: The layout automatically adapts so both players see the screen right side up.
  • Fast & Fun: Most rounds finish in under 1 minute.
  • Pure Privacy: No accounts, and works 100% offline without Wi-Fi.
